OVERVIEW AND CONTRIBUTIONS
==========================

This work addresses a critical challenge at the intersection of formal methods 
and deep reinforcement learning: zero-shot generalization of LTL (Linear 
Temporal Logic) specification satisfaction. We propose HiLTL, a novel 
hierarchical neuro-symbolic framework that overcomes two fundamental 
limitations in existing approaches:

1. SEMANTIC FLATTENING: Standard GNNs treat automaton transitions uniformly, 
   losing crucial semantic distinctions (epsilon-transitions, forward progress, 
   shortcuts, and acceptance loops). Our Semantically-Enhanced GNN (SE-GNN) 
   explicitly encodes transition types and node roles through edge-conditioned 
   attention, enabling the network to discover context-dependent aggregation 
   strategies.

2. TEMPORAL MYOPIA: Existing methods use static global embeddings that fail to 
   adapt to task progress. We introduce a novel Hierarchical Value Decomposition 
   that separates credit assignment across temporal scales: short-term pathways 
   optimize dense immediate rewards and local constraints, while long-term 
   pathways focus on sparse acceptance signals. Dynamic fusion via a 
   proximity-based gate adaptively balances both pathways.

EMPIRICAL RESULTS AND SIGNIFICANCE
===================================

Comprehensive experiments on three continuous control benchmarks (PointLtl2-v0, 
LetterEnv, FlatWorld) demonstrate:
- +6.4% success rate improvement over strong internal baselines
- Consistent gains across easy, medium, and hard LTL specifications
- Strong performance on both finite-horizon and infinite-horizon tasks
- Zero-shot generalization to unseen formulas
- Improved sample efficiency and trajectory quality

These improvements validate that hierarchical temporal decomposition provides 
a general principle for credit assignment in logic-guided RL, particularly on 
complex specifications.

ORIGINALITY AND NOVELTY
=======================

To our knowledge, this is the first work to:
1. Systematically encode automaton transition semantics in GNNs via edge-
   conditioned attention for LTL-guided RL
2. Decompose value functions hierarchically across temporal scales aligned 
   with automaton progress
3. Introduce a proximity-based dynamic fusion gate that adapts credit 
   assignment based on task progress

While hierarchical RL and GNN semantics have been studied separately, their 
principled integration for LTL satisfaction is novel and well-motivated by 
the structure of logical specifications.
